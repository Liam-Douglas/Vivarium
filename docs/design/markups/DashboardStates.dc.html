Five states, one layout

A queue-led dashboard has an obvious failure mode: on a well-kept collection the queue is empty most days, and on long feeding cycles “most days” is nearly all of them. The fix is not to pad the screen — it is to make the slots hold something true in every state.

The false all-clear — worth fixing whatever else happens here

Today getStatusForAnimal returns 'muted' when an animal has no last_fed_at or no feeding_frequency_days, and the overdue count tallies only 'red'. So an animal with no schedule is invisible to every queue in the app, permanently — and a collection of twelve animals that has never been fed renders “Overdue 0” in green.

The dashboard’s healthiest state and its least-known state currently look identical. Any real all-clear design has to be able to tell them apart before it is allowed to say “nothing due”.

The four slots — fixed in every state

1 Workload line busy — “2 overdue · 2 due today” quiet — “Nothing due today · next Thursday”
2 Queue card busy — “Today”, actions on every row quiet — “Coming up”, dates instead of actions
3 Attention card busy — hidden — the queue outranks it quiet — “Worth a look”, max 4 rows
4 Stock + activity busy — amber warning + shopping list quiet — a two-week projection

Slots never move and never disappear; their heading and fill change. A quiet day reads as a calm version of the same screen rather than a different app — and the busy day is protected too, because “Worth a look” is capped and yields entirely when there is a queue.

The states

State Trigger Queue slot shows What happens today
New household No animals One onboarding card — add an animal, or import a collection Already handled — “Add your first animal”
No history yet Animals exist, none has a logged feed “Set a feeding schedule” — the setup the collection still needs Reads as healthy: “Overdue 0” in green, “Fed this week 0 of 12”
All clear Everything scheduled, nothing due within 3 days “Coming up” — the next 7 days, dated, no actions Grid of every animal + three vanity stats fill the space
Quiet, with gaps Nothing due, but some animals unscheduled or long unweighed “Coming up”, with “Worth a look” leading on the unscheduled animals Indistinguishable from all clear
Busy Anything overdue, due today, or a dose scheduled “Today” — the ordered queue with actions Split across “Needs feeding” and “Due soon”

Two of the five states are ones the app cannot currently distinguish, and both of those are the quiet ones. That is the real answer to “what if everything is up to date”: the dashboard should be able to tell you that it is genuinely up to date, rather than that it has nothing to say.
