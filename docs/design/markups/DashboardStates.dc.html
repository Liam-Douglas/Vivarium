Five states, one layout

A queue-led dashboard has an obvious failure mode: on a well-kept collection the queue is empty most days, and on long feeding cycles “most days” is nearly all of them. The fix is not to pad the screen — it is to make the slots hold something true in every state.

The false all-clear — fixed

The bug: getStatusForAnimal returned 'muted' when an animal had no last_fed_at or no feeding_frequency_days, and the overdue count tallied only 'red'. Such an animal was invisible to every queue in the app, permanently, and twelve animals that had never been fed rendered “Overdue 0” in green. They mostly arrive by bulk import: the form defaults a blank frequency to 7, but the importer writes null.

The fix: lib/feedingStatus.ts is now the single source of truth — 'muted' splits into 'no-schedule' and 'never-fed', both counted apart from 'on-schedule'. The Overdue tile is green only when every animal is tracked, and an amber notice names the ones that are not.

The design consequence stands either way: an all-clear message is only honest once the screen can tell those states apart, which is why “animals with no feeding schedule” leads “Worth a look” rather than sitting at the bottom of it.

The four slots — fixed in every state

1 Workload line busy — “2 overdue · 2 due today” quiet — “Nothing due today · next Thursday”
2 Queue card busy — “Today”, actions on every row quiet — “Coming up”, dates instead of actions
3 Attention card busy — hidden — the queue outranks it quiet — “Worth a look”, max 4 rows
4 Stock + activity busy — amber warning + shopping list quiet — a two-week projection

Slots never move and never disappear; their heading and fill change. A quiet day reads as a calm version of the same screen rather than a different app — and the busy day is protected too, because “Worth a look” is capped and yields entirely when there is a queue.

The states

State Trigger Queue slot shows What happens today
New household No animals One onboarding card — add an animal, or import a collection Already handled — “Add your first animal”
No history yet Animals exist, none has a logged feed “Set a feeding schedule” — the setup the collection still needs Fixed — Overdue tile reads amber, “of 0 tracked”, and the notice names them
All clear Everything scheduled, nothing due within 3 days “Coming up” — the next 7 days, dated, no actions Grid of every animal + three vanity stats still fill the space
Quiet, with gaps Nothing due, but some animals unscheduled or long unweighed “Coming up”, with “Worth a look” leading on the unscheduled animals Fixed — the unscheduled animals are named on the dashboard
Busy Anything overdue, due today, or a dose scheduled “Today” — the ordered queue with actions Split across “Needs feeding” and “Due soon”

The two states the app could not distinguish were both quiet ones, and that half is now shipped: the dashboard can say whether it is genuinely up to date rather than merely having nothing to report. What is still open is the rest of this page — a quiet day currently fills with the animal grid and the vanity stats rather than with “Coming up” and “Worth a look”.
