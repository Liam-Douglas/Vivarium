Build Order

Six phases to ship the design markups, ordered by what unblocks what rather than by screen. Each phase stands alone — you can stop after any of them and the app is coherent.

Three defects found while planning

All three are in Phase 1. They are independent of whether you build any of the redesign, and the first one loses data silently.

  1. 01Enclosure batch feed does not deduct feeder stock. handleBatchFeed in Animals.tsx still uses the legacy createFeedingLog + updateAnimal pair, while single feeding goes through the atomic log_feeding RPC. Feeding six animals from a rack writes six logs and deducts nothing, so stock silently drifts — and the two writes are not atomic. Found today.
  2. 02Notifications disagree with the screens. isOverdue in lib/dates.ts treats days >= freq as overdue; every screen uses > freq. An animal at exactly its interval gets a push saying “overdue” while the dashboard shows “Due soon”.
  3. 03The profile turns amber a day early. AnimalDetail.tsx uses freq - 2 where the shared status module uses freq - 1.

Seven decisions for you

None of these block starting Phase 1. Each has a recommendation; say the word and I take it, or overrule any of them.

Which destination gets the fifth bottom-nav tab?

Recommend Feeding and Stats in, Expenses under More. Expenses currently takes a quarter of the phone’s navigation for a screen you open monthly. If your household actually checks spend weekly, swap it with Stats.

Delete the two orphaned pages, or wire them into the nav?

Recommend delete. Feeder stock already lives inside Expenses and the feeding calendar redirects to the Feeding page — keeping 854 lines of unreachable code that duplicates shipped screens costs more than it saves.

Is an animal overdue at freq days, or after it?

Recommend after — align notifications to > freq. That matches every screen today; the alternative moves four screens instead of one. It does mean push notifications fire a day later than they do now.

Do feeding streaks survive?

Recommend cutting the card badge, keeping the Stats section. On a 171px card it is one of six competing pieces of metadata; on Stats it is a thing you went looking for.

Removing the dashboard’s all-animals grid — is your household happy with that?

The one change in Phase 2 that removes something people may use daily. It renders every animal, so it is also the single biggest cause of dashboard scroll. Worth asking before it goes.

Batch feed: loop the existing RPC, or add a new one?

Recommend looping log_feeding. No migration, atomic per animal, and a rack is rarely more than a dozen. A log_feedings RPC would be one round trip and one transaction, but it is the only thing in this plan that needs a schema change.

Add a test runner?

Recommend vitest before Phase 6. There is none today, so the 19 checks behind the all-clear fix ran as a throwaway script. Phase 6 is all date arithmetic across five states — the first work here where that genuinely hurts.

What this does not cover

Expenses, Import, Settings and the auth screens are untouched — they were outside the markups and nothing above depends on them. Nothing here needs a Supabase migration: medication_logs, medication_schedules, enclosures and the feeder_stock view all exist already, and the only work at the data layer is making three query functions accept a missing animalId. The one exception is the optional log_feedings RPC in decision six.
